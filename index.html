WHY RELOOP EXISTS

Useful things deserve
another chance.

MCC ReLoop is a campus reuse community built to make responsible material circulation easier at Madras Christian College. By helping people pass on surplus items to someone who can use them, ReLoop encourages practical reuse, reduces avoidable waste and extends the life of everyday things.
